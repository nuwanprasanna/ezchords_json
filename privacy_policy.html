Privacy Policy - EZ Chords

Last updated: August 24, 2026

EZ Chords ("we", "our", or "us") operates the EZ Chords mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our application.

1. Age Requirement

EZ Chords is intended for users aged 13 years and older.

Users under the age of 13 should not use the application or provide personal information through the application.

2. Information We Collect

EZ Chords is designed to provide chord and music-related content.

We do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address

Personal information may only be provided if you voluntarily contact us.

3. API and Content

EZ Chords retrieves chord and music-related content from the following API:

https://nuwanprasanna.github.io/ezchords_json/

The content provided by this API is used to provide features and information within the EZ Chords application.

4. Third-Party Services

EZ Chords may use third-party services required for application functionality, including Google services and advertising services if enabled.

Third-party services may collect technical information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the application. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

EZ Chords is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
